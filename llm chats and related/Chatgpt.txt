# 🥫 SIH26236 — AI-Based Intelligent Food Packaging Material Recommendation System

This PS is basically asking you to build an **AI-powered decision-support system that tells a food producer what packaging material and specifications they should use for a particular food product.**

It is from the **Ministry of Food Processing Industries (MoFPI)** under **Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development**.

---

## 1. What is the problem in simple words?

Different foods need different packaging.

For example:

* Potato chips need protection from **oxygen and moisture**
* Fresh vegetables need to **breathe**
* Frozen food needs packaging that works at **low temperatures**
* Oil-rich food may need strong protection against **oxidation**
* Fruits may require controlled **O₂ and CO₂ transmission**

Today, choosing the correct packaging often depends heavily on **expert knowledge and manual analysis**.

Small food businesses, farmers and startups may not know:

> "Which material should I use for my product?"

This PS wants software that answers that question intelligently.

# 6. The system must recommend more than material

This is where this PS becomes interesting.

The output isn't just:

> "Use LDPE."

It should recommend **packaging specifications**.

The PS specifically lists:

### OTR

**Oxygen Transmission Rate**

How easily oxygen passes through the packaging.

### WVTR

**Water Vapor Transmission Rate**

How easily water vapour passes through the packaging.

### Film thickness

How thick the packaging material should be.

### Sealability

How effectively the package can be sealed.

### Gas permeability

How gases move through the packaging.

### Mechanical strength

How well the packaging can withstand handling and transportation.

### MAP suitability

Whether the packaging is suitable for **Modified Atmosphere Packaging**.

---

# 7. Fresh fruits and vegetables are particularly interesting 🍅🥬

This is one of the strongest parts of the PS.

Fresh produce is still **respiring after harvest**.

In simple terms:

```text
Produce consumes O₂
        ↓
Produces CO₂
        ↓
Packaging must allow appropriate gas exchange
```

If you completely seal some fresh produce in an inappropriate package, gas composition can become unsuitable and spoilage can increase.

Therefore the system should consider:

**Respiration rate + packaging permeability**

and recommend:

* Breathable films
* Micro-perforated films
* Suitable gas exchange
* MAP conditions

The PS specifically calls for considering respiration rate and recommending breathable/micro-perforated packaging and gas composition for MAP.

---

# 8. What is MAP?

### Modified Atmosphere Packaging

Instead of simply putting food inside normal air, the atmosphere inside the package is controlled.

For example:

```text
Normal air
O₂ ≈ 21%
CO₂ ≈ 0.04%
```

A MAP system might use a different gas composition depending on the food.

Your software could therefore output something like:

```text
Recommended packaging:
Breathable film

MAP suitability:
YES

Target gas composition:
O₂ → recommended range
CO₂ → recommended range
```

**Important:** the actual gas composition should come from your verified food-packaging dataset/research rather than arbitrary numbers.
